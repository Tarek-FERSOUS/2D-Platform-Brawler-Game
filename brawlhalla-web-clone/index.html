P1: A/D move, W jump, S fast-fall, J light, K heavy, L dodge

P2: Arrow keys move/jump, Numpad 1 light, Numpad 2 heavy, Numpad 3 dodge

Press B to toggle Bot control for Player 2.

Press M to rotate to the next stage.

Collect glowing orbs for temporary powers: shield, float, speed, heavy, throwables, explosives.

Teleport gates and moving platforms are active on advanced stages.
